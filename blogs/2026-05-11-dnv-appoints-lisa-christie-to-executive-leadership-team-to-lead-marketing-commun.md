DNV has appointed Lisa Christie as a new member of the Energy Systems Executive Leadership Team heading Marketing, Communication & Public Affairs (MCP). She is expected to take up the role on 7 September 2026 and will report directly to Energy Systems’ CEO Ditlev Engel. In this position, she will lead the global MCP unit in Energy Systems and help strengthen DNV’s market positioning, stakeholder engagement and public affairs capability in a period marked by major geopolitical and regulatory change.
